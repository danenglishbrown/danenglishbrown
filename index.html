04 / About

A little more context

The person behind the terminal.

I study theoretical physics at King's College London and have spent much of the last few years turning mathematical ideas into working software.

That has meant building an electromagnetic simulator, modelling options and market-neutral portfolios, developing finance software, teaching physics and mathematics, and helping younger students build and program a competition robot.

I am most useful when the problem sits between disciplines — when understanding the theory matters, but so does making the final thing clear, robust and usable.

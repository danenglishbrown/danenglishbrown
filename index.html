02 / QUANTUM COMPUTING

QAOA for Max-Cut.

Quantum optimisation · Qiskit

I implemented the Quantum Approximate Optimisation Algorithm (QAOA) for the Max-Cut problem in Python using Qiskit, explicitly constructing the variational quantum circuit and classical optimisation loop rather than relying on a black-box implementation.

I compared COBYLA, SLSQP and Powell across complete and sparse graphs, examining convergence, approximation quality and the effect of increasing circuit depth. Powell was the strongest and most consistent optimiser in my experiments.

Problem
Solving Max-Cut with QAOA

03 Investigation of Quantum Topological Code Python · Qiskit · TensorNetwork · Quantum Error Correction Summer 2027 +

My MSc dissertation with Dr Po-Shen Hsin at King's College London will examine topological quantum codes — the error-correcting structures that make fault-tolerant quantum computation possible. It will cover stabilizer and CSS codes, the surface and colour codes, and the theory of error correction and decoding. The latter half extends the framework to cluster states and measurement-based quantum computing, with the aim of constructing codes and optimising their properties.

I will be writing my dissertation from Jun 2027 — Sept 2027.
